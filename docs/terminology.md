# Controlled terminology

The manuscript, figures, supplementary material and Atlas use the following
terms consistently.

| Concept | Required term | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General socioeconomic and demographic setting | **social conditions** | Narrative statements that do not refer to the constructed ordering |
| Prespecified composite variable, `V` | **social-condition ordering** | The three-domain ordering used for typology and allocation |
| Discretised `V1`-`V3` class | **social-condition tier** | Lower, middle or upper tier |
| Objective, ranking rule or allocation based on `V` | **social-context objective**, **social-context-first rule** or **social-context allocation** | Allocation analyses |
| ACS racial-share variable | **Not White alone** | Defined as 100 minus the ACS White-alone racial share |
| Context-relative canopy shortfall, `G` | **canopy deficit** | Typology and allocation analyses |
| Equal weighting of the thermal and social objectives | **illustrative equal-weight rule** | Diagnostic allocation comparison, not a recommended optimum |

`Social constraint`, `social-condition-first`, `canopy-gap first`, `balanced
rule` and `people of colour` are not used as public labels because they either
overstate the construct, obscure its definition or do not match the ACS
variable definition. The raw field names `people_of_color_pct`,
`social_constraint` and `canopy_gap` are retained only as internal
compatibility keys and must never be displayed.
